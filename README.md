# Direct Dataset

**[directdataset.com](https://directdataset.com)**

Direct Dataset is an independent provider of public data. It gathers publicly available information on property markets, retail prices and consumer products and supplies it as documented, record-level datasets: one row per transaction, contract, listing, price or review.

## Free datasets

Small parts of the full datasets, in the same columns, free to download as Parquet and CSV.

| Free dataset | What is inside | Full dataset |
|---|---|---|
| [Sephora US products](https://huggingface.co/datasets/directdataset/sephora-us-products-free-dataset) | 1,000 beauty products: prices, ratings, badges and categories | [Sephora US Product Catalogue and Price History](https://directdataset.com/datasets/sephora-us-product-catalogue) |
| [Tesco UK grocery products](https://huggingface.co/datasets/directdataset/tesco-uk-grocery-products-free-dataset) | 500 grocery products with prices, offers, ingredients, nutrition and allergens | [Tesco UK Product Catalogue](https://directdataset.com/datasets/tesco-uk-product-catalogue) |
